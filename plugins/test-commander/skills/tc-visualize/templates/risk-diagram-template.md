# Risk register

Risk register grouped by severity. Generated by `/tc:diagram-risk`; every node is a recorded risk and never invents a risk or severity.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    subgraph sev_high["High severity"]
        risk_risk_<n>["RISK-<n>: <Area>"]
    end
    subgraph sev_medium["Medium severity"]
        risk_risk_<n>["RISK-<n>: <Area>"]
    end
```
